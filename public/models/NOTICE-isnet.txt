isnet_general_q.onnx — background removal model used by Unmark

Based on IS-Net (DIS: Highly Accurate Dichotomous Image Segmentation),
general-use weights, by Xuebin Qin, Hang Dai, Xiaobin Hu, Deng-Ping Fan,
Ling Shao and Luc Van Gool.
  https://github.com/xuebinqin/DIS
ONNX export distributed by rembg:
  https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg

Quantized by Unmark (dynamic uint8 weights; see scripts/quantize-isnet.py).
